Strategy ⟷ Execution

Strategy is naming the real problem and the shape of the answer. Execution is building it so it holds up without me. Most people are anchored to one end. I work the full height, which is why I can spot the problem and still be the one who fixes it.

GTM ⟷ Operations

I came up in go-to-market, then moved horizontally into the operational layer underneath it. Same operator, wider surface. That range is why I can connect a pipeline problem to the process and tooling actually causing it.
